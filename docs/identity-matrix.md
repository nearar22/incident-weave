# IncidentWeave identity check

| Dimension | IncidentWeave | GrantPath | CiteGuard | RegTrace |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary object | Operational incident | Grant application | Citation audit | Regulatory review |
| Decision material | Multi-source status chronology | Applicant-to-criteria fit | Pinpoint source support | Policy change evidence |
| Consensus output | Ordered events plus conflict graph | Pass, fail, and missing readiness map | Citation findings | Review findings and appeal |
| State transition | Add one witness, resynthesize, seal | Revise applicant profile, reassess, finalize | Appeal an audit | Appeal a review |
| Deterministic derivation | Incident phase from events and conflicts | Overall readiness from criteria | Audit lifecycle | Review lifecycle |
| Interface | Dark signal scope and incident rail | Warm editorial application path | Citation workspace | Regulatory trace workspace |

IncidentWeave does not reconcile disagreement into a single convenient narrative. Cross-source conflicts are first-class on-chain objects connected to exact quoted events.
